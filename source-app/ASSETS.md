# Asset provenance

`public/images/editorial.webp`, `shirt.webp`, `kurta.webp`, `overshirt.webp`, and `bag.webp` derive from two original image-generation requests made for this project. They depict illustrative fashion, not actual inventory or brand partnerships. The original editorial and contact-sheet PNGs are retained in the same folder. No third-party logos or product photography were copied.

The four manufacturer labels are fictional sample content: Studio North (demo), Rang Studio (demo), and Mysa Goods (demo). Avika Collection remains the retailer identity.

Replace sample photographs with owner-supplied or properly licensed actual product photos before real trading. Uploaded files are the owner's responsibility; the form accepts only PNG/JPEG/WebP and does not assert image rights or product authenticity.

Interface icons are from the installed Lucide React package; typography uses local/system Georgia and Arial/Helvetica without external font requests.
